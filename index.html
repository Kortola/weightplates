About Us

At our shop in Crown Point, Indiana, we believe gym equipment should be built to last, built to perform, and built around your needs.

We specialize in commercial grade, custom built gym equipment, including plate loaded machines, selectorized equipment, and strength training machines designed for serious use. Whether you're outfitting a commercial gym, upgrading your training facility, or building your dream home gym, we can bring your vision to life.

Your Equipment. Your Way.

We don't believe in a single standard design. Every piece of equipment can be customized to fit your needs, including:

  • Custom sizes to fit your space and training goals.
  • Custom colors to match your gym's style or brand.
  • Personalized branding with your gym's name or logo.
  • Plate loaded and selectorized designs built for strength, durability, and performance.
  • Fast turnaround times without compromising quality.

From a single custom machine to an entire gym setup, we focus on delivering equipment that's built exactly the way you want it.

American Made. Built With Pride.

Every piece of equipment we build is proudly made in the USA, right here in Crown Point, Indiana.

We believe quality craftsmanship starts with valuing the people behind it. Everyone involved in bringing our equipment to life earns a livable wage and receives health, dental, and vision insurance, along with pension benefits.

When you choose our equipment, you're doing more than investing in American made quality. You're supporting American manufacturing, skilled craftsmanship, fair wages, and good jobs with meaningful benefits.

Built in America. Built with pride. Built by people who are valued.

Local Delivery. Personal Service.

We proudly serve customers throughout Indiana, Illinois, Wisconsin, and Michigan, offering free delivery on qualifying orders within a 100 mile radius of Crown Point, Indiana.

We believe buying custom gym equipment should be straightforward. From discussing your ideas and choosing your specifications to building and delivering your equipment, we're committed to providing personal service, fast turnaround times, and quality you can count on.
